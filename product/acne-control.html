Niacinamide

Effective for management of Hyper-Pigmentation

How it worksinhibits the transfer of melanosomes from melanocytes to the surrounding skin cells (keratinocytes) by 35% to 68%

Niacinamide acts on a cellular level to protect the extracellular matrix and reverse apparent signs of aging

How it worksIt acts as a precursor to the endogenous antioxidants NADH and NADPH, which fuel DNA repair, boost collagen production, and block the spontaneous oxidative cross-linking (glycation) that yellows aging skin

Clinical research shows that niacinamide deeply restores compromised or dry skin

How it worksIt upregulates the synthesis of epidermal ceramides, free fatty acids, and cholesterol in keratinocytes, preventing transepidermal water loss (TEWL)

It exhibits sebostatic and anti-inflammatory properties that calm the swelling and redness caused by inflammatory acne.

  • *4% nicotinamide gel is of comparable efficacy to 1% clindamycin gel in the treatment of acne vulgaris. Because topical clindamycin, like other antimicrobials, is associated with emergence of resistant microorganisms, nicotinamide gel is a desirable alternative treatment for acne vulgaris.

Zinc PCA

Topical Zinc PCA (Zinc Pyrrolidone Carboxylic Acid) is a highly effective, multifunctional active ingredient designed specifically for oily, acne-prone, and sensitive skin types. Unlike traditional zinc salts, which can dry out or irritate the skin, Zinc PCA couples a therapeutic zinc ion with PCA—a naturally occurring humectant that constitutes roughly 12% of the skin's Natural Moisturizing Factor (NMF)

Zinc PCA regulates oil production directly at the cellular level

How it worksIt acts as a potent inhibitor of the 5-alpha-reductase enzyme. This enzyme converts testosterone into dihydrotestosterone (DHT), the primary androgen hormone responsible for stimulating and enlarging sebaceous glands

  • Consistent topical application of Zinc PCA results in a 30% decrease in sebum production within 6 weeks, noticeably reducing surface shine without dehydrating the epidermis

Zinc PCA provides broad-spectrum antimicrobial defense against the specific microorganisms responsible for inflammatory skin conditions.

Azelaic Acid

antibacterial, anti-inflammatory and reducing excessive keratinization.

its anti-infective and anti-inflammatory effects. AZA is a competitive inhibitor of mitochondrial oxidoreductases and 5α-reductase, inhibiting the conversion of testosterone to 5-dihydrotestosterone

Azelaic acid (AzA) is defined as a topical agent effective in the treatment of papulopustular rosacea, demonstrating significant reductions in inflammatory lesions and erythema severity compared to placebo and being equally effective as metronidazole in clinical studies.

antibacterial, keratolytic, depigmenting, antioxidant, and anti-inflammatory effects.

AZA is FDA-approved for treating PPR, and research supports its use in melasma and as a second-line treatment for acne vulgaris.

How it worksThe molecular mechanism of action of AZA is broad, one of which is thioredoxin inhibition. The thioredoxin enzyme regulates tyrosinase activity through a feedback mechanism involving electron transfer to intracellular thioredoxin, followed by a specific interaction between reduced thioredoxin and tyrosinase [14, 15]. In addition, the thioredoxin/thioredoxin reductase system has been shown to be a major electron donor for DNA-regulating ribonucleotide reductases [16]. The inhibition of thioredoxin reductase by AZA provides a rationale for both its depigmenting properties and the reversible inhibition of DNA synthesis observed in cultured epidermal cells, as well as in some bacteria associated with acne vulgaris.

Mandelic Acid (AHA) 0.5%

Mandelic acid, a lipophilic alpha hydroxy acid, reduces lipid production, enhances exfoliation and provides clinical and patient perceivable benefits to oily and photodamaged skin

Mandelic acid is defined as a peeling agent that penetrates the skin gradually and uniformly, making it suitable for sensitive skin. It has anti-inflammatory effects and is often used in combination peels for conditions such as melasma, with minimal side effects and quick reepithelization.

Salix Alba (Willow Bark) Extract

Gentle Keratolytic / Exfoliant: Promotes the desquamation (shedding) of the stratum corneum. Unlike synthetic salicylic acid, it accelerates cell turnover without disrupting the lipid barrier or causing skin flaking.

Astringent: High natural tannin content cross-links surface proteins, which temporarily tightens skin tissue, reduces excess sebum output, and minimizes the appearance of pores.

Antioxidant (Radical Scavenger): Rich in flavonoids (such as quercetin and rutin) and polyphenols that neutralize reactive oxygen species (ROS) induced by UV radiation and pollution
