Saw Palmetto Extract

Oil extracted from saw palmetto berries ( Serenoa repens ) contains 80%–85% fatty acids and some minor components like sterolic alcohols [4]. Unlike finasteride's active‐site inhibition, lipidosterolic extracts of Serenoa repens inhibit both type I and II 5‐α‐reductase via non‐competitive (allosteric) mechanisms, an effect largely attributed to free fatty acids in the extract [5]. Niacinamide

Niacinamide Down-Regulates the Expression of DKK-1 and Protects Cells from Oxidative Stress in Cultured Human Dermal Papilla Cells

Niacinamide significantly decreases the protein expression of Dickkopf-1 (DKK-1). DKK-1 is a notorious inhibitor of the Wnt/β-catenin pathway that forces hair follicles out of the growth phase and drives premature entry into the catagen (shedding) phase

Niacinamide acts on a cellular level to protect the extracellular matrix and reverse apparent signs of aging

How it worksIt acts as a precursor to the endogenous antioxidants NADH and NADPH, which fuel DNA repair, boost collagen production, and block the spontaneous oxidative cross-linking (glycation) that yellows aging skin

Clinical research shows that niacinamide deeply restores compromised or dry skin

How it worksIt upregulates the synthesis of epidermal ceramides, free fatty acids, and cholesterol in keratinocytes, preventing transepidermal water loss (TEWL)

Arginine

Arginine deficiency accelerates androgenetic alopecia. Serum metabolome shows reduced circulating arginine, while local metabolic disruption depletes follicular reserves, triggering reactive oxygen species (ROS) accumulation and mTOR suppression that induce hair miniaturization. Topical arginine delivery via microneedles reverses this process, demonstrating therapeutic potential.

Combination of arginine and zinc tested in our study could represent a good therapeutic option for the treatment of AGA and TE and it might represent a valid alternative to finasteride.
