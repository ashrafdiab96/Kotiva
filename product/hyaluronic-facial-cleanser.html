The ScienceActives & clinical references

Hyaluronic Acid 1%

How it worksTopical hyaluronic acid (HA) acts primarily as a high-capacity humectant, binding up to 1,000 times its weight in water to the skin surface to provide immediate hydration, plumping, and smoothing effects. It works by forming a breathable, moisture-retaining film, decreasing transepidermal water loss (TEWL) while reducing fine lines and enhancing skin elasticity.

  • Enhances hydration
  • Supports collagen function and skin structure

Topical hyaluronic acid (HA) is scientifically proven to significantly increase skin hydration (up to 134% immediately, ~55% at 6 weeks) and improve elasticity.

Pro-Vitamin B5 (Panthenol)

How it workspanthenol penetrates the stratum corneum and is converted into pantothenic acid (vitamin B5), an essential component of coenzyme A. This coenzyme plays a pivotal role in various biochemical pathways, including those involved in lipid metabolism and energy production. At the cellular level, pantothenic acid contributes to the synthesis and maintenance of fatty acids and sphingolipids, which are crucial for the integrity and function of the skin barrier.​

It Has Hygroscopic Humectant: Structurally features a high count of hydroxyl (-OH) groups, allowing it to rapidly attract, bind, and lock water molecules into the outermost skin cells during the washing process

Surfactant Mitigator / Anti-Irritant: Cushions the skin against the harsh cleansing action of anionic surfactants (such as Sodium Laureth Sulfate), preventing them from excessively stripping intercellular ceramides

Non-Comedogenic & Soothing: Delivers significant soothing benefits without forming an occlusive, heavy, or pore-clogging film, making it highly compatible with oily, acne-prone, and ultra-sensitive skin type
