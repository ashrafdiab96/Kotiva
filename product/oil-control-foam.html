Allantoin

Allantoin functions as a multi-stage cell-regenerating agent. It speeds up the skin's transition from an inflammatory state to a proliferative phase, making it a staple for wound healing, scar reduction, and severe barrier repair

Has anti-inflammatory and antioxidant effects. It has been shown to repair cutaneous photodamage and reduce inflammation following UVR exposure.

How it worksIt operates by strictly regulating the localized inflammatory response, directly stimulating fibroblastic proliferation, and accelerating the synthesis of the extracellular matrix (dermal scaffolding)

Has Keratolytic Activity: It acts as a chemical debrider by melting the intercellular cement holding dead cells together, removing necrotic tissue, and clearing hyperkeratotic changes (roughness).

Pro-Vitamin B5 (Panthenol)

How it workspanthenol penetrates the stratum corneum and is converted into pantothenic acid (vitamin B5), an essential component of coenzyme A. This coenzyme plays a pivotal role in various biochemical pathways, including those involved in lipid metabolism and energy production. At the cellular level, pantothenic acid contributes to the synthesis and maintenance of fatty acids and sphingolipids, which are crucial for the integrity and function of the skin barrier.​

Hygroscopic Humectant: Structurally features a high count of hydroxyl (-OH) groups, allowing it to rapidly attract, bind, and lock water molecules into the outermost skin cells during the washing process

Surfactant Mitigator / Anti-Irritant: Cushions the skin against the harsh cleansing action of anionic surfactants (such as Sodium Laureth Sulfate), preventing them from excessively stripping intercellular ceramides

Non-Comedogenic & Soothing: Delivers significant soothing benefits without forming an occlusive, heavy, or pore-clogging film, making it highly compatible with oily, acne-prone, and ultra-sensitive skin type

Salvia Officinalis (Sage) Extract

Anti-Inflammatory and Antioxidant Properties

Sage extracts downregulate the expression of key pro-inflammatory cytokines, specifically Interleukin-6 (IL-6), Interleukin-1β (IL-1β), and Tumor Necrosis Factor-alpha (TNF-α). Ursolic acid within the plant interferes with the NF-κB transcription factor, stopping the signals that trigger localized cellular swelling and pain

improves wound healing process

Sage triggers a multi-step cellular repair sequence. It upregulates the genetic expression of Fibroblast Growth Factor-2 (FGF-2) and Vascular Endothelial Growth Factor (VEGF). This forces rapid cellular multiplication, stimulates angiogenesis (new blood vessel growth), and increases hydroxyproline—the primary structural building block of skin collagen

Sage (Salvia officinalis) extract enriched for ursolic acid clinically reduces skin sensitivity to irritants and lightens the appearance of facial hyperpigmentary spots

highly effective in treating mild-to-moderate acne and post-acne scarring

Lactic acid works by decreasing cellular cohesion.
